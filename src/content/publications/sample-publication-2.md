abstract: "We analyze the spectral characteristics of wind-generated waves in the tropical Pacific using a combination of satellite altimetry, buoy measurements, and WAVEWATCH III model simulations. The study reveals distinct regional patterns in wave spectra related to trade wind variability and ENSO conditions. Our findings improve understanding of wave climate variability in this critical oceanic region."
