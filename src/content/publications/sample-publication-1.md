abstract: "This study investigates wave-current interactions in coastal environments using high-resolution numerical simulations. We employ the SWAN wave model coupled with the ROMS circulation model to examine how tidal currents affect wave propagation and dissipation in a shallow coastal region. Results show significant modulation of wave heights and directions due to current refraction, with implications for coastal sediment transport and morphodynamics."
