## Project Overview

This research project was part of the ERC HIGHWAVE project and developed novel wavelet-based methods for analyzing ocean wave directional spectra. Traditional methods often rely on parametric assumptions about the shape of the directional distribution, which may not capture the full complexity of real ocean wave fields.

## Key Objectives

1. Develop data-driven methods for directional spectrum estimation
2. Validate techniques against field observations
3. Compare with traditional parametric methods
4. Apply to long-term GPS buoy datasets

## Methodology

The project utilized:
- **Continuous Wavelet Transform (CWT)** for time-frequency analysis
- **GPS buoy measurements** from the west coast of Ireland
- **Machine learning** for pattern recognition
- **Statistical validation** against known sea states

## Main Findings

- Wavelet methods provide superior resolution in frequency-direction space
- Non-parametric approaches better capture bimodal and complex sea states
- Results published in *Journal of Atmospheric and Oceanic Technology*
