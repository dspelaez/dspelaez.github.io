## Project Overview

The ARANSAT project, funded by CNES (Centre National d'Études Spatiales), focuses on analysing directional wave spectra using data from the CFOSAT satellite mission. By collocating satellite observations with measurements from Spotter buoys and NDBC (National Data Buoy Center) buoys, we aim to validate and improve our understanding of wave directionality from space-borne instruments.

## Objectives

- Collocate CFOSAT observations with in-situ buoy measurements
- Analyse the shape and characteristics of directional spectra from multiple data sources
- Validate satellite-derived directional information against ground truth
- Assess the performance of CFOSAT's wave scatterometer (SWIM) for directional spectrum estimation

## Methodology

The project combines:
- **CFOSAT SWIM data**: Satellite-based wave measurements
- **Spotter buoys**: GPS-based wave monitoring systems
- **NDBC buoys**: Traditional moored buoy observations
- **Statistical analysis**: Comparison and validation of directional distributions

## Significance

This work contributes to:
- Improved validation of satellite wave products
- Better understanding of directional wave field variability
- Enhanced calibration of remote sensing instruments
- Development of operational satellite-based wave monitoring systems
